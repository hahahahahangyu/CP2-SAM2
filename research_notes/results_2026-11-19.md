# Results: full cloud run, 2026-11-19

Run on an NVIDIA L40S (`results/gpu.txt`). The benchmark took 5.9 min; the NPD night check took a few minutes.
Tables are in `results/tables/` (point-prompt night check) and `results/tables_nightbox/` (box-prompt night check).
Figures are in `results/figures/`, regenerated with `python scripts/figures.py --example pothole600__0002_p0`.

## Sanity check

- mean IoU 0.653 (per-pothole bootstrap CI 0.623–0.680), median 0.719
- 42% of potholes at IoU ≥ 0.75, 19% at IoU < 0.5
- size counts 8 / 96 / 92 / 10

## Findings

1. **SAM degrades in the dark.** The paired drop from daylight is:

   | Level | Drop in IoU [95% CI] |
   |---|---|
   | Mild | −0.077 [−0.113, −0.042] |
   | Medium | −0.061 [−0.096, −0.025] |
   | Severe | −0.132 [−0.168, −0.092] |

   All three are significant after Holm correction. Mild and Medium are not significantly different from each other (+0.016 [−0.005, 0.039], Wilcoxon p = 0.95), so treat that range as a plateau.

2. **Enhancement helps only at Severe.** Difference from plain SAM at the same level (paired):

   | Method | Mild | Medium | Severe |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | CLAHE | −0.031 | −0.018 | +0.034 [0.025, 0.044] |
   | Gamma | −0.021 | −0.008 | **+0.046 [0.034, 0.058]** |
   | Zero-DCE | −0.023 | −0.011 | +0.034 [0.021, 0.046] |

   - At Mild, all three enhancers are significantly worse than plain SAM.
   - At Medium, none is significantly different from plain SAM (Holm p ≥ 0.71).
   - At Severe, all three are significantly better. Gamma is the best fix and recovers about a third of the drop.

3. **RobustSAM** is the best method in daylight (+0.054 [0.031, 0.077] over SAM), but the worst in the dark.
   - It under-segments: its median predicted/GT area falls from 0.96 in daylight to 0.38 at Severe, and 10.7% of its Severe masks are empty.
   - Plain SAM fails the other way. It over-segments, spilling outside the box over the noisy road: its median area ratio rises from 0.87 to 1.68.
   - Figure 5 shows both failure modes.
   - The RobustSAM wrapper matches the official `eval.py` line for line, so this is model behaviour, not a wrapper bug.

4. **Noise ablation** (`scripts/ablation_noise.py`, `results/ablation_noise.csv`). Severe darkness with the noise switched off:

   | Method | Severe + noise | Severe, no noise | Day |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | SAM | 0.521 | 0.652 | 0.653 |
   | RobustSAM | 0.309 | 0.352 | 0.707 |

   SAM's drop comes from **sensor noise**, not from darkness. RobustSAM's drop comes from the darkness itself.

5. **Size.**
   - Small potholes (n = 96) lose the most: 0.72 → about 0.51 at Severe.
   - The tiny (n = 8) and large (n = 10) bins are too small to draw conclusions from (`small_n` flag in `by_size.csv`).
   - RobustSAM falls to an IoU of 0 on tiny potholes in the dark.

6. **Real night (NPD test).** 280 dark images (luma < 0.35), 509 boxes. The ranking does **not** match the simulation.
   - With a centre-point prompt, box IoU vs SAM:

     | Method | Difference vs SAM [95% CI] |
     |---|---|
     | CLAHE | +0.020 [0.002, 0.037] (the only enhancer that helps) |
     | Gamma | −0.052 |
     | Zero-DCE | −0.037 |

   - With a box prompt (`--box-prompt`, scored on fill), plain SAM fills the most (0.468). The enhancers are about 0.44 and RobustSAM 0.415.
   - Discuss this as a gap between the simulated and the real night.

## Caveats for the report

- **RobustSAM has no single-point support.** Its point-prompt NPD score (0.020; 90.6% empty masks) is not a night result. It returns empty masks for single-point prompts even in daylight: 74% empty, IoU 0.10, against 0.33 for SAM (`scripts/diag_robustsam_prompts.py`). multimask_output makes no difference. Use the box-prompt night run to compare RobustSAM.
- **Severe sits at luma 0.087–0.089, not 0.081 (NPD p25).** The noise floor prevents it within Lv et al.'s γ range (see `configs/levels.json`).
- **NPD limitations.** The images are Roboflow 640×640 stretches, and the night check is scored at box level only.
- **Darkening model.** The CBDNet noise model uses the sRGB curve as the camera response, with no Bayer or JPEG step.
- **Darkness occasionally helps SAM.** On the 5 cracks-and-potholes images (10 potholes), SAM scores higher at Severe than in daylight (0.42 → 0.58).
